BRAND WEBSITE

EIHAMBA COFFEE

A story-led site for Bwindi coffee that protects mountain gorillas.

← All projects
SectorSpecialty coffee & conservation
What we didBrand storytelling, Website design, Next.js development, Custom domain
StartedApr 2026
Last updatedMay 2026
Live siteeihambacoffee.org

The brief

Eihamba Coffee sources premium Ugandan coffee from the Bwindi region, working with local farming communities. The story of conservation matters as much as the product.

What we built

A long-form, image-rich site that opens with "Every Cup Protects Mountain Gorillas", then walks through services, impact, the coffee itself, products, the journey from cherry to cup, the leadership team and partners.

How it went

  1. Apr 2026Project started: brief agreed, structure and design direction set.
  2. BuildDeveloped with Next.js, Vercel.
  3. May 2026Latest release shipped and deployed on Vercel.
  4. TodayLive at eihambacoffee.org.

Key features

  • Impact statistics and conservation storytelling
  • Coffee sourcing, processing and export services
  • Product and local product ranges
  • Leadership team and partner sections
  • Contact and enquiry forms
