Engage places in top 3% of UK PPC agencies

31st March 2026

Roughly a 1 minute read by

James

rich-text

We’re officially a Google Premier Partner! 

It’s a status we’ve held since the programme first launched in 2022, consistently placing us in the top 3% of PPC agencies in the UK - a group that represents fewer than 200 agencies nationwide.

To earn and retain this, agencies need to meet Google’s highest standards, including:

  • Maintaining advanced certifications and optimisation standards
  • Demonstrating expertise across Search, Display, Video and more
  • Managing significant ad spend across accounts
  • Retaining clients with sustained performance and growth

In short, it’s a mark of consistent, high-quality delivery at scale.

Delivering real results for our clients

For us, this recognition reflects the work happening behind the label.

Our paid media team manages accounts across a wide range of sectors, from FMCG to hospitality and retail, all with one common focus: helping clients maximise ROI on their paid media investment. 

The success we’ve delivered for our clients is reflected in the team’s continued growth, too. In 2025, we onboarded seven new paid media clients and appointed two new heads to strengthen the team further.

pull-quote

Google Premier status reflects the performance we deliver for our clients, the rigour behind our optimisation and the ambition we bring to every account. For our clients, that means a partner they can trust to lead strategically, while maintaining high standards in the day-to-day delivery.

James Elliman, Engage

Head of Paid Media
