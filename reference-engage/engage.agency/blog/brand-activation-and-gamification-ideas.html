Brand Activation Game Ideas to Level Up Your Brand

12th September 2023

Roughly a 9 minute read by

Matt

rich-text

Brand activation games can turn a nice campaign idea into something people actually want to take part in. Useful, really. By adding game mechanics, interactive challenges and clear rewards into your marketing, you can create brand experiences that feel more memorable, measurable and genuinely engaging.

Traditional campaign formats still have their place, but audiences are very good at ignoring anything that feels passive, predictable or like it has wandered straight out of a 2014 marketing deck. Gamification gives people something to do, not just something to look at.

From spin-the-wheel games and quizzes to referral challenges, in-app activations, competitions and seasonal campaigns, we’ve pulled together six gamification ideas to help brands drive participation, collect useful data, build loyalty and make their next activation work a bit harder.

Activation game ideas at a glance

Short on time? Here are the six brand activation game ideas covered in this guide:

  1. Use spin-the-wheel games to create a simple, prize-led interaction.
  2. Create quizzes that entertain people while collecting useful audience insight.
  3. Add referral challenges to turn customers into active brand advocates.
  4. Build in-app activations that connect the experience to an existing digital journey.
  5. Use competitions and challenges to drive participation and a bit of healthy rivalry.
  6. Tap into seasonal moments with timely games, offers and campaign hooks.

The best brand activation games are not just fun for the sake of it. They give people a clear reason to take part, make the experience easy to understand and help the brand collect something useful along the way. Ideally not just screenshots of people spinning a wheel, but we’ll take what we can get.

How to choose the right game for your brand activation

The best brand activation games start with the goal, not the mechanic. A spin-the-wheel game might work for quick prize-led engagement, while a quiz can help gather audience insight. Referral challenges, in-app activations and seasonal competitions all have their place, but they work best when they support a clear campaign objective.

Before choosing the format, think about what you want people to do next. Are you trying to collect data, drive sign-ups, increase dwell time, boost referrals or create more participation at an event? Once that’s clear, the right game mechanic becomes much easier to choose, whether you’re planning a simple competition or a more bespoke digital activation.

6 activation ideas for your brand marketing strategy

1. Spin the wheel games

Imagine a world where marketing strategies aren’t just about selling but also about having a blast. Well, welcome to the realm of spin-the-wheel games! These nifty little activations bring a playful twist to your brand’s marketing strategy. With a simple spin, your audience gets a shot at winning some fantastic prizes, exclusive discounts, or special surprises. It’s like injecting a dose of fun and excitement into your brand’s presence.

But wait, there’s more! Beyond the laughs and thrills, spin-the-wheel games also help you gather valuable customer data. It’s a win-win situation where your customers enjoy the experience, and you gain insights into their preferences.

2. Get quizzical

Who doesn’t love a good quiz? Quiz games are an excellent addition to your brand marketing strategy, offering a delightful way to engage and interact with your audience. By creating quizzes that align with your brand or industry, you can entertain and educate your customers while subtly showcasing your expertise. These interactive games spark curiosity and challenge participants to test their knowledge, creating a sense of excitement and friendly competition.

Quiz games also allow you to gather valuable insights into your customers’ preferences and interests, as you can tailor the questions to align with your target demographic. Plus, sharing quiz results and comparing scores fosters a sense of community among your audience.

3. Chase those referrals

Referrals are the secret sauce of brand activations, and they’re a game-changer for your marketing strategy. Why? Because there’s nothing more powerful than good old word-of-mouth. When someone enthusiastically recommends your brand to their inner circle, it carries incredible trust and authenticity. People listen to recommendations from their friends and family.

You tap into this influential force by incorporating referral programs into your brand marketing strategy. Your satisfied customers become your brand advocates, spreading the word and bringing in new customers. It’s like having a team of passionate supporters cheering you on. Referrals generate leads and build a vibrant community around your brand. Jet2 is a perfect example of this: our Window Wonderland campaign generated 43% of website traffic via referral.

4. In-app activations

In-app activations are a vital component of your brand marketing strategy. By integrating interactive features and exclusive rewards within your app, you engage users, collect valuable data, and create a seamless brand experience that keeps them returning for more. It’s a game-changer for building customer loyalty and driving your marketing goals forward. We worked with the online travel agency Kiwi.com to develop a digital activation and competition to promote their holidays within the Uber app. This initially launched across Poland and Romania, (intending to upscale across other regions in the future) and generated a huge 12,000 database opt-ins! 5. Add a splash of competition

Competition activations are a dynamic addition to your brand marketing strategy. Introducing contests, challenges, or competitions ignites a spirit of friendly rivalry among your audience. This gamified approach captivates participants and generates buzz and excitement around your brand.

Competitions encourage active engagement, foster a sense of community, and can lead to increased brand visibility and customer loyalty. Take our internal Elympics for example, this type of healthy competition sparks our colleagues to participate actively, resulting in more money for our sponsored charities.

6. Tapping into the seasonal spirit

Seasonal activations bring freshness and excitement to your brand marketing strategy. By aligning your campaigns with specific seasons, holidays, or events, you can tap into your audience’s collective enthusiasm and anticipation. Whether it’s a holiday-themed game, limited-time offers, or special promotions, seasonal activations create a sense of urgency and FOMO.

It’s an opportunity to infuse your brand with a seasonal vibe and connect with your customers on a deeper level. One of our recent seasonal activations was the Ryobi ONE+ Cup campaign, specifically targeted at the World Cup event. This proved to be a highly successful campaign, scoring a huge 34,000 sign-ups!

Ready to build a better brand activation?

Gamification can make a brand activation more engaging, more measurable and far more useful than a standard campaign mechanic. The trick is making sure the game serves the idea, the audience and the outcome. Otherwise, it’s just a wheel with some branding on it. Fun, but not exactly strategic.

Whether you’re planning a spin-the-wheel game, quiz, referral mechanic, in-app experience, seasonal campaign or something more bespoke, our digital activations team can help shape the idea, build the experience and make sure it works properly for the people using it.

FAQs

Why use games for brand activations?

Games can help brand activations stand out, especially when audiences are used to ignoring standard campaign messages. A good game gives people a simple action, a reason to engage and a reward for taking part. It can also help brands collect useful data, increase dwell time and drive sign-ups or referrals.

What are examples of interactive brand activations?

Examples of interactive brand activations include spin-the-wheel prize games, branded quizzes, referral challenges, in-app competitions, motion-based games, leaderboard challenges and seasonal digital campaigns. The best format depends on the campaign goal, audience and channel. As always, the slightly annoying answer is: it depends.

What makes a good brand activation game?

A good brand activation game is simple to understand, easy to play and clearly connected to the campaign. It should not feel like a random game with a logo slapped on top. The mechanic, reward and user journey should all support the brand objective.

How can gamification help brands collect data?

Gamification can help brands collect data by encouraging users to take part in a quiz, competition, prize draw or interactive challenge. In exchange for a useful or entertaining experience, users may share preferences, contact details or campaign responses. The important bit is making the value exchange clear, not sneaky.

Can gamification work for seasonal campaigns?

Yes, seasonal gamification can be very effective when it links to a clear moment, event or behaviour. Holiday games, countdowns, tournaments, prize draws and limited-time challenges can all create urgency and encourage participation. Basically, give people a reason to care now, not eventually.
