B-Corp

Let's B better

We’ve always had a strong sense of wanting to do the right thing. Not just in digital, but also in the impact we have in the world. That’s why we are beyond proud to be a Certified B Corporation™.

“You’re a B what?” we hear you ask.

B Corp is a new kind of business model that balances purpose and profit - a community of organisations that meet the highest standards of verified social and environmental performance, public transparency and legal accountability. 

By becoming accredited, we now have a more organised and measured approach to the issues that are important to us. 

What’s more, it aligns us with similarly minded people in businesses who, in our opinion, are also driven to do the right thing.

We know that Engage is going to be a fantastic addition to our community and will continue driving the conversation forward.

Chris Turner

Executive Director of B Lab UK

There are already 430 B Corps™ in the UK, employing over 24,000 people in 48 industries, with a combined revenue of £4.3 billion. By becoming a B Corp™, we’ve joined a growing number of companies in this country and beyond who not only believe there’s more to business than profit, they are prepared to be legally bound to their commitments.

We might not be able to change the world overnight, but we can all make a start to become a (B)etter (Corp)oration.
