About us

rich-text

We’re Engage, the digital agency for those that demand attention.

Founded in the mighty Leeds back in 2007, we've grown into one of the UK’s most reputable digital agencies. And we’re not just us saying that; industry bods like Prolific North and The Drum both think so too. 

100% employee-owned, we’re personally invested in every project. Your success is our success, and we bring the full weight of our expertise, creativity, and commitment to every challenge. No rotating cast - just a tight-knit team of experts who care deeply about doing great work and delivering real results.

As the UK’s highest-rated B Corp™ digital agency, we believe in using business as a force for good. From our hands-on volunteering programme to our award-winning Elympics initiative, we create impact where it counts. 

Few deliver like we do: world-class technical expertise, seamless scalability, and an Attention to Digital™ that keeps brands growing, and audiences, well... engaged!

This level of attention cannot be automated. Neither can we.

Agency values? 

Let’s be honest, most sound the same.

Clear communication? Of course. Transparency? We’d hope so. Honesty and integrity? Surely that’s the bare minimum. So when it came to defining our own, we knew they had to mean more. They had to feel uniquely us - not something you could copy, paste, and slap on another website.

These values are how we hire, how we work, how we hold ourselves to a higher standard - and how we deliver digital that actually makes a difference. They’re not just words on a page. They show up in the way we treat each other, partner with clients, and push every project that little bit further.

Here's what we live by:

Attention to Digital™

Attention to Digital™ is our stamp of quality; a promise to sweat the small stuff, obsess over craft, and earn trust through care, clarity and considered detail.

Own it!

Own it! is our mindset. We take initiative, speak up, and give a damn; about our work, our clients, and each other. Because this business is ours.

Be the glue

Great work starts with strong relationships. Ours are built on empathy, trust and care. The kind that connects teammates and clients alike. Projects come and go, but strong relationships hold it all together. That’s what sets us apart.

Proudly employee owned

We’re proud to be a 100% employee-owned agency, which means everyone here has a real stake in your success. With us, there's no revolving door of talent - just an expert team that’s all in, every time.
