Rebecca Walker

Why the dog icon?

My dog Brie has never failed to put a smile on my face. Although she’s a genuine menace and once cost a fortune at the vets for secretly eating a full bag of dog food, I wouldn’t change her for the world. She represents always looking on the bright side and making people happy.

A bit more about Rebecca

What do you enjoy most about your role?

I love how varied the work is. One day you're working on content for a fashion brand, the next you're defining structured data for a national restaurant brand. Every single day is different.

What challenges do you enjoy solving?

There’s no one-size-fits-all approach to SEO, so the fun challenge is digging into each client’s website and projects to uncover the biggest opportunities to hit their brief!

Which project at Engage are you proudest of, and why?

Can I say the Engage site? It's been just great having that full team buy-in and getting to be part of how we represent ourselves online.

Which Summer Conference has been your favourite, and why?

Palma - my first one! I hadn't even started working here yet but, by the end of the weekend it felt like I'd been here for years!

What’s the most valuable lesson you’ve learned in your career?

You can’t really break a website. Just test, learn, and iterate rather than overthink things.
