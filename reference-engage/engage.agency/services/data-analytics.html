Make your data work as hard as you do

A data analytics agency that transforms stats into success

Numbers alone don’t build brands – insights do. There’s data everywhere you look, but what sets successful marketers apart is the ability to interpret, connect and act on that information. As an agency dedicated to excellence in data analytics, we transform raw information and numbers into strategic marketing intelligence, ensuring every decision taken is backed by real, actionable insights. We don’t do anything that isn’t informed, intentional or impactful.

We build integrated data ecosystems that connect your marketing channels – SEO, PPC, social, CRM and more – into one cohesive view. This clear, 360° view of performance allows us to uncover hidden opportunities and connect the dots across the funnel. From advanced attribution modelling to server-side tracking, we ensure your data is accurate, compliant and primed for smarter decision-making.

But we don’t stop at tracking performance – we actively enhance it. With predictive analytics, AI-powered automation and real-time optimisation, we transform fragmented data into forward-looking strategies. Whether it’s fine-tuning campaigns in real time or identifying the next growth opportunity before it happens, we keep your brand one step ahead.

With Engage, data isn’t just a reporting tool – it’s your strategic superpower making for faster, smarter decisions. We use it to give your marketing activity sharper focus and measurable impact.

Why choose Engage?

Data that works – from collection to activation

Siloed data leads to missed opportunities. Our multi-channel marketing analytics framework unites SEO, PPC, social and CRM insights, giving you a full-funnel view of audience behaviour and marketing performance. Using Google BigQuery, SQL and custom analytics scripts, we turn fragmented datasets into absolute strategic clarity.

Multi-channel analytics that connect the dots

We go beyond tracking data – we engineer it for impact. From server-side tracking and first-party data strategies to data lakes and warehouses, we build scalable infrastructures that fuel SEO, paid and CRM strategies. Every data point is structured, governed and primed for actionable insights.

Smarter automation, sharper optimisation 

We automate, predict and perfect. From AI-powered bid strategies to custom attribution modelling and real-time reporting dashboards, our solutions ensure marketing decisions aren’t just informed by data – they’re driven by it.

 

A data-led approach

Advanced tracking & data collection infrastructure

We build custom GA4, server-side tracking and API integrations, ensuring your data collection infrastructure is accurate, scalable and primed for insight-driven marketing.

Marketing performance analysis & attribution modelling

We map multi-touch attribution models, blending organic, paid and CRM data to pinpoint what’s driving conversions – and where to make improvements.

Big data processing & governance

Using Google BigQuery, SQL and data lakes, we structure raw data into clear insights, giving you a deeper understanding of trends and performance.

Automated insights & data visualisation

We create real-time dashboards in Looker Studio and Power BI, turning complex data into actionable intelligence for marketing teams and stakeholders.

Predictive modelling & AI-driven optimisation

Machine learning and AI-powered modelling predict trends, dynamically adjust bids and fine-tune audience targeting for better performance at scale.

Data pipeline automation & API integrations

We build automated workflows that sync with paid media, CRM and analytics platforms – eliminating time-consuming manual work and speeding up decision-making.

Compliance & data security management

Your data is secure, compliant and privacy-first. We ensure GDPR adherence, ethical data handling and airtight governance while maximising insights.
