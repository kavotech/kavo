As a leading AI SEO agency, we help brands earn that attention. AI search doesn't replace SEO. It builds on it. That's why we don't treat SEO and GEO as competing disciplines. We bring them together in one joined-up strategy, built around today's search behaviours to strengthen your digital authority and make your brand easier to discover wherever your customers search.

FAQs

What is AI SEO?

AI SEO is the practice of improving your visibility across both traditional search engines and AI-powered search platforms. Alongside helping your website rank in Google, it focuses on increasing the chances of your brand being cited, recommended or referenced by tools like ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, Gemini and Claude.

What is Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO)?

Generative Engine Optimisation, or GEO, is the process of making your website easier for AI platforms to understand, trust and reference. That includes creating helpful content, strengthening technical SEO, improving structured data and building the authority signals that AI models rely on when generating answers.

Is AI SEO replacing traditional SEO?

Not at all. If anything, good SEO is more important than ever. AI platforms rely on many of the same signals that search engines have always valued, including high-quality content, technical performance and website authority. GEO builds on those foundations, helping your brand become more visible across AI-powered search as well as traditional search results.

Which AI platforms do you optimise for?

We optimise for the platforms that are shaping how people search today, including ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity. Rather than chasing platform-specific tactics, we focus on strengthening the underlying signals that help your content perform consistently across AI search.

How do you measure AI search performance?

Traditional SEO focuses on rankings, traffic and conversions. AI SEO adds another layer, measuring how often your brand is seen, cited and recommended in AI-generated responses. Alongside your existing SEO reporting, we'll help you understand how visible your brand is across AI search and where there are opportunities to improve.

How do I know if my brand appears in AI search?

Most businesses simply don't know, and that's exactly where we start. We use our bespoke AI Discoverability Analyser to show how your brand appears across a range of AI search journeys - where you're visible, where competitors have the edge and the practical steps that can improve your presence.

Do I need AI SEO if I already invest in traditional SEO?

Probably. If you already have a strong SEO strategy, you're in a great position because many of the foundations are already in place. AI SEO builds on that investment, helping ensure your content is ready for the growing number of people who are turning to AI for recommendations, comparisons and advice.
