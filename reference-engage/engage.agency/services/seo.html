FAQs

How long does it take for SEO to show results?

SEO is a long-term strategy, and results can vary depending on competition, industry, and current website health. Typically, noticeable improvements can be seen within 3-6 months, with more significant growth over time.

What’s the difference between SEO and Pay-Per-Click (PPC)?

SEO focuses on organic rankings by optimising your website and content, leading to long-term visibility. PPC delivers immediate traffic through paid ads but requires ongoing budget investment. A strong digital strategy typically combines both.
