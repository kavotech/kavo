Digital PR: building brand authority

A digital PR strategy that puts you in the headlines

A successful digital PR strategy isn’t one that just puts the attention on you. It’s one that actually makes an impact on your success. At Engage, we know for a fact that all the clicks, likes and views in the world don’t mean anything for your brand if they don’t drive engagement in the real world.

Our digital PR service creates campaigns that work by aligning what we do with your goals, inserting you into important online conversations and helping you to own the space you’re in. From strategy through to delivery, we explore what is going to get you noticed, what will build brand momentum and what combines reputation enhancement with tangible results.

Our aim is to get you the backlinks that really count for something. The articles that position you where you want to be. The headlines that work harder for you than a thousand adverts. We’ll grow your authority and reputation in a way that feels organic and earned, delivering a lasting brand boost that will work wonders in tandem with all your other marketing activities.

With Engage as your digital PR agency, you won't just get attention. You’ll get the right kind of attention.

Why choose Engage?

Many hands, one goal

Our in-house digital PR & performance teams work seamlessly together with our other departments to maximise results. Collaboration drives achievement at Engage, from planned digital PR campaigns to fast-moving social media responses. We move as one to give you more momentum.

Performance-driven PR with purpose 

Data-backed insights, smart keyword research and strategic storytelling underpin creative digital PR strategies that hit hard and deliver real results. Our performance team digs deeper to turn your brand’s story into a masterpiece of measurable success.

Making the most of media 

From digital activations to videos and infographics that work as attention magnets, our design and content teams bring stories to life in ways that drive backlinks, brand expansion and conversation. The shareable, interactive stuff that the media loves? We create it all in-house.

Our Digital PR services:

News generation campaigns

We’ll work closely with you to develop ideas and stories that will catch the eyes of the media - and improve your brand’s reach and reputation. Whether it’s a research-backed campaign or finding a new way to cut through the noise, we do it all.

Activations & interactive tools

We create interactive tools and engaging activations that don’t just react to the news - they become the story. Whether it’s a quiz, data-driven tool or reaction test, we build unique assets that provide the impetus for coverage and conversation.

SEO content strategy

We create data-driven content roadmaps that match search intent. From blog posts to landing pages, our SEO content strategy strengthens topical authority and keeps your brand visible and relevant.

Reactive PR

There’s nothing worse than a brand chiming in on a trend long after it has run its course. We make sure that doesn’t happen by staying ahead of the conversation, seizing those moments where your brand can add value and shine - and not just for the sake of it.

Digital PR performance tracking

Coverage is nice, impact is better. We track how digital PR moves the needle for SEO. Links, rankings, visibility - all that good stuff that means real results for your brand.

Multi-channel amplification

Here, PR isn’t just PR. We reach further by integrating campaigns and PR hooks into on-page SEO, paid ads and social for a multi-channel boost that maximises your visibility and impact.
