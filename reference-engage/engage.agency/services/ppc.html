FAQs

How quickly can PPC deliver results?

PPC can start driving traffic as soon as campaigns go live. While performance improves over time through testing and optimisation, it is often one of the fastest ways to generate measurable results. 

Does PPC work alongside SEO and other channels?

Yes. PPC works best as part of a wider digital strategy. It can support SEO by capturing demand quickly, testing messaging and filling visibility gaps, while also complementing paid social, CRO and CRM activity.

How do you control PPC budgets?

Budgets are actively managed and continuously reviewed. We monitor spend, performance and efficiency closely, adjusting bids, targeting and creative to make sure your budget is working as hard as possible.

 

What platforms do you manage PPC campaigns on?

We manage PPC across platforms including Google Ads, Microsoft Advertising and programmatic display networks. Campaigns can include search, shopping, display and remarketing, depending on where your audience is most active.

Is PPC right for my business?

PPC works well for most businesses, but the approach should reflect your goals, market and margins. Whether you are looking to drive immediate sales, generate leads or support brand visibility, PPC can be tailored to suit your commercial objectives.
