Why choose Engage?

Built for your brand, not pulled from a shelf

Every activation we create is original and unique - designed from scratch to reflect your goals, your audience and your tone of voice. Whether it’s a mobile-first campaign for Gen Z or a flash promotion for peak season, we tailor every detail in-house across strategy, design and dev.

Reward mechanics that motivate

We build value into the journey. That could mean instant wins, exclusive offers or surprise-and-delight gifting - whatever drives your audience to act. It’s reward logic designed to increase participation, boost conversion and give users a reason to come back.

Strategy that supports the full funnel

Our activations aren’t standalone - they’re part of your wider performance ecosystem. From CRM triggers to voucher integrations and user segmentation, we design with your sales funnel in mind. Everything is measurable. Everything has a purpose. Everything is designed to sell.

FAQs

Do I need to already have a CRM in place?

No. We can set one up with you or plug into whatever you already use.

Can you help design the rewards?

Absolutely. From planning and sourcing to integrating the right incentives for your audience, we’ve got it covered.

How fast can we go live?

Most digital activation campaigns launch within 6-14 weeks, depending on scope. We’ll build a timeline that works for you and aligns with your other marketing.

What data do I get back?

Everything from user preferences and engagement rates to voucher redemptions - fully GDPR compliant and built to inform your next move.
