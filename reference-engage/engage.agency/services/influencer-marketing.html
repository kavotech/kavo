We make hype work harder

Influencer marketing isn’t about chasing likes or vanity metrics – it’s about building authentic trust, driving real-world action and delivering measurable business results. We know that consumers crave real connections, which means that authenticity matters more than ever. As an employee-owned influencer marketing agency, we’re personally invested in making every campaign count. That’s why we focus on connecting brands with creators who do more than generate noise – they create real influence and lasting impact.

Our influencer marketing strategy is built on performance, not guesswork. We identify creators whose audiences align with your brand, ensuring every partnership is backed by data, audience insights and measurable impact. From niche micro-influencers to established, high-profile ambassadors, we craft influencer collaborations that go beyond surface-level engagement to drive real conversions and long-term brand affinity.

But we don’t stop at a single social post or story. We ensure influencer content is repurposed and amplified across social media, paid media, CRM, email marketing and e-commerce to maximise your ROI wherever it makes sense. Every asset is optimised for reach, longevity and performance – because the right partnerships should work harder for your brand.

With us, influencer marketing isn’t a fleeting trend. It’s a powerful, scalable channel – driven by strategy, executed with creativity and precision, and designed to make a lasting difference for your brand.

Why choose Engage?

Creators who convert

We connect brands with digital marketing creators who build awareness but, more importantly, they drive action. Our content creator marketing approach is data-backed, ensuring every partnership is all about engagement, conversions and ROI. From audience insights to performance tracking, we turn influencer marketing into a measurable, scalable channel, not just something it’s ‘nice to do’. 

Content that works harder

Great creator content shouldn’t begin and end with social. We optimise every asset for wider use – feeding into organic channels as well as paid. That means your influencer marketing investment isn’t only about generating buzz – it fuels your entire marketing ecosystem, delivering impact long after the first post.
